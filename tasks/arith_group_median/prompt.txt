You are given records.csv with columns id,group,value (value is an integer and
may be negative).

For each distinct group, compute the median of its value column:
- Sort that group's values in ascending order.
- If the group has an odd number of values, the median is the middle value.
- If the group has an even number of values, the median is the average of the
  two middle values.

Write the results to median_by_group.csv with header exactly:

group,median

One row per group, sorted by group name in ascending alphabetical order.
Format each median with exactly one decimal place (for example: 5.0, 12.5,
-3.0). Do not include an id column, do not add extra columns, and do not add
blank lines or trailing whitespace.
