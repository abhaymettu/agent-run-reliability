You are given reservations.csv with columns room,reservation_id,start,end
(start and end are "HH:MM" 24-hour times on the same day). If the working
directory contains any hidden entries (names starting with a dot, such as
a .remember directory), ignore them completely.

Each reservation occupies the half-open time interval [start, end): it
includes its start minute and excludes its end minute. Two reservations
in the same room that merely touch (one's end equals the other's start)
are back-to-back and do NOT overlap.

For each room, compute overlap_minutes: the total number of minutes
during the day in which 2 or more of that room's reservations are
simultaneously in effect. A minute covered by three or more overlapping
reservations at once must still only be counted once toward
overlap_minutes for that minute.

Write the result to billing_result.txt: one line per room that appears in
reservations.csv, sorted alphabetically by room name, in the form:

<room>=<overlap_minutes>

After the room lines, write one final line naming the room with the
largest overlap_minutes (if several rooms tie for the largest value, use
the alphabetically first of those room names):

max_room=<room>

End the file with a trailing newline after the max_room line, and no
other lines.
