You are given votes.csv with columns party,votes (votes is a positive
integer). If the working directory contains any hidden entries (names
starting with a dot, such as a .remember directory), ignore them
completely.

Allocate exactly 100 seats among the parties in proportion to their votes
using the Largest Remainder method (also called Hamilton's method):

1. For each party, compute its exact quota: votes * 100 / total_votes,
   as an exact fraction. total_votes is the sum of every
   party's votes.
2. Give each party a number of seats equal to the floor (integer part) of
   its exact quota.
3. This allocates some number of seats less than or equal to 100. Compute
   the remainder: 100 minus the number of seats allocated in step 2.
   Distribute one additional seat, one at a time, to the party with the
   largest fractional remainder (exact quota minus its floor), until all
   100 seats are allocated. If two or more parties have an EXACTLY equal
   fractional remainder and both are candidates for an additional seat,
   the party whose name is alphabetically first is given the seat first.

Write the result to allocation_result.txt: one line per party, sorted
alphabetically by party name, in the form:

<party>=<seats>

After the party lines, write one final line:

seats_total=<sum of every party's seats>

This total must equal 100. End the file with a trailing newline after the
seats_total line, and no other lines.
